BREAKING LANGUAGE BARRIERS: HOW TRANSLATION DRIVES ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI) FORWARD

Author: Professor Salisu Ahmad Yakasai, FLAN

Date: 20/02/2026

Absract

Translation in Artificial Intelligence (AI) is super crucial for breaking language barriers and making into accessible globally. However, language and cultural barriers pose significant challenges to effective communication and cooperation. The paper explores the critical role of translation in breaking language barriers with a view to driving Artificial Intelligence (AI) forward. We study the challenges and solutions to translation challenges in Artificial Intelligence (AI). Our analysis highlights the future trends in translating Artificial Intelligence (AI). We conclude that translation in Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a game-changer for global communication.
