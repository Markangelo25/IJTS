📢Call for Papers - IJTS Volume 4.1 (2027)

The Ilorin Journal of Translation Studies (IJTS), a peer-reviewed publication of the Institute of Translation Arts, University of Ilorin, Nigeria, invites original scholarly contributions for Volume 4.1 (2027).

We welcome theoretical, empirical, applied and interdisciplinary research in Translation and Interpreting Studies, including artificial intelligence, machine translation, multilingualism, terminology, lexicography, indigenous languages, literary translation and intercultural communication.

📅Submission Deadline: 30 November 2026

📖Publication Date: 31 January 2027

🌍 Languages of Publication: Arabic, English, French, German, Hausa, Igbo and Yorùbá.

Submission Requirements: APA 7th Edition; abstract of 150–250 words; 4–7 keywords. All manuscripts undergo editorial screening and double-blind peer review.

🔗 Author Guidelines: https://www.ijts.site/submission.html

IJTS — Languages | Cultures | Knowledge | A More Connected World

Submission and Review Process

Contributors should send their manuscripts to ijts@unilorin.edu.ng with copies sent to the email addresses listed in the Contact section below. Articles should range from 4,000 to 8,000 words, while book reviews should not exceed 2000 words. All submissions should adhere to APA style sheet (6th Edition). Manuscripts submitted to IJTS undergo an initial editorial assessment before being sent for a double-blind peer review by two reviewers. Book reviews do not undergo double-blind peer review but are reviewed by the editors. A submission fee of N6,000 (Six thousand naira only) covers the review process. Accepted papers would attract a publication fee of N25,000 (twenty-five thousand naira only).
